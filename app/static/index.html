Sprout System Help & User Guide

How to navigate environments, log documentation, and maintain zero data contamination.

1. Understanding Sprout Spheres (Environments)

Sample Caregiver Sprout (Dev Sandbox - Blue Header): Safe testing ground pre-loaded with 15 synthetic caregiver households, 20 children in care, sample FS-221A milestones, and training logs. Zero live data is queried or exposed.

Local Caregiver Sprout (Local Live Data - Emerald Header): Operational environment for Candy Chevalier, Mallory Wheeler, and Robin Davis. Restricts homes strictly to 41 Elmwood Dr and 22 East Parkside, with CD as Potential Placement.

2. Selecting a Caregiver User

The top-right Caregiver User dropdown changes your active identity. When launching in sandbox, it defaults to -- Select Sample Caregiver --. Choosing a caregiver instantly scopes:

  • Households: Scoped strictly to that caregiver's home and licensing stage.
  • Children In Care: Scoped strictly to youth placed in that home.
  • Timeline & Binders: Displays realistic synthetic logs (pediatrics, IEPs, visits).
  • Compliance (FS-221A): Displays the checklist tailored to that home's approval stage.

3. Fast 15-Second Documentation & DCF Drafts

Click Log Note (or the fast action buttons on the Dashboard) to record pediatric visits, school IEP notes, family time interactions, and behavioral observations. The system automatically drafts formal notification emails for your DCF Family Services Worker and GAL based on Vermont policy requirements.

4. Local & Offline Privacy Guarantee

All files and SQLite databases run 100% locally on your computer with zero external cloud transmission. In Sample Sandbox mode, private OneDrive files are completely shielded so personal information (SSNs, court orders) is never exposed.
