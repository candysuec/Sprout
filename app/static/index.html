Vermont Kinship Fast-Track Guide Plain-English Survival Guide

Demystifies welfare bureaucracy: immediate triage, financial benefits, urgent paperwork, and what can wait.

Days 1 – 3 Triage Essentials:
  • Medical Authorization Form: Ask the DCF caseworker for the signed medical consent form right away so you can take the child to urgent care or fill prescriptions without delay.
  • School Placement Letter: Obtain DCF educational placement documentation so the child remains in their current school or enrolls immediately without residency issues.
  • Safe Sleep & Predictable Meals: Ensure the child has their own safe bed and establish consistent meal and sleep rhythms to stabilize stress.
  • Log Everything in Sprout: Use the + 15 Second Log to jot down arrival condition, medication times, and caseworker promises.
Reach Up (Child-Only Cash Grant)

Monthly cash grant (~$350–$550/mo per child) paid directly to relative kin. Does not count your caregiver income—based solely on the child's status.

Dr. Dynasaur (Medicaid)

100% free healthcare, dental, vision, mental health, and prescription coverage for all children in foster or kinship care in Vermont.

Child Care Subsidy (CCFAP)

State financial assistance covering up to 100% of licensed daycare and afterschool program costs for working kinship caregivers.

Family Support Fund ($350/yr)

Annual state flexible grant for clothes, winter gear, school supplies, or sports fees. Reimbursed through your DCF family services worker.

🔴 Urgent (Within 48 Hours): DCF Medical Consent Form, Emergency Placement Agreement, emergency worker cell phone number.
🟡 Important (Within 14 Days): Reach Up child-only application to Economic Services, School residency notification, scheduling the 30-day EPSDT health screening.
🟢 Ongoing (Within 30 Days): Fingerprint background check, FS-221A Home Safety Walkthrough with licensing worker.
De-Escalate Pressure (You Do NOT Need To Worry About These Today):
  • You do NOT need to decide on permanent adoption or guardianship right now. Focus only on today's safety and comfort. Permanency planning unfolds over months.
  • You do NOT need to complete 30 hours of foster training immediately. Kinship caregivers have extended grace periods for classes.
  • You do NOT need all brand new bedroom sets. A safe bed, dresser space, and clean bedding are all that the safety walkthrough requires.
  • You do NOT need to hire private lawyers for DCF court. The child has an appointed attorney (GAL / Public Defender) who handles formal motions.
CHINS ("Children in Need of Care or Supervision"):

The official Vermont court case opened to ensure child safety and connect the family with support services.

EPSDT ("Early & Periodic Screening, Diagnostic, & Treatment"):

Vermont's free comprehensive 30-day well-child medical exam covering physical, dental, vision, hearing, and mental health checkups.

RPPS ("Reasonable and Prudent Parent Standard"):

Vermont state law empowering you as a caregiver to say "yes" to normal childhood activities (field trips, sports, sleepovers) without asking DCF for permission.

Reach Up:

Vermont's cash assistance program providing monthly child-only financial grants to relative caregivers.

Dr. Dynasaur:

Vermont's 100% free comprehensive Medicaid coverage for all children in foster and kinship care.

Sprout System Help & User Guide

How to navigate environments, log documentation, and maintain zero data contamination.

1. Understanding Sprout Spheres (Environments)

Sample Caregiver Sprout (Dev Sandbox - Blue Header): Safe testing ground pre-loaded with 15 synthetic caregiver households, 20 children in care, sample FS-221A milestones, and training logs. Zero live data is queried or exposed.

Local Caregiver Sprout (Local Live Data - Emerald Header): Operational environment for Candy Chevalier, Mallory Wheeler, and Robin Davis. Restricts homes strictly to 41 Elmwood Dr and 22 East Parkside, with CD as Potential Placement.

2. Selecting a Caregiver User

The top-right Caregiver User dropdown changes your active identity. When launching in sandbox, it defaults to -- Select Sample Caregiver --. Choosing a caregiver instantly scopes:

  • Households: Scoped strictly to that caregiver's home and licensing stage.
  • Children In Care: Scoped strictly to youth placed in that home.
  • Timeline & Binders: Displays realistic synthetic logs (pediatrics, IEPs, visits).
  • Compliance (FS-221A): Displays the checklist tailored to that home's approval stage.

3. Fast 15-Second Documentation & DCF Drafts

Click Log Note (or the fast action buttons on the Dashboard) to record pediatric visits, school IEP notes, family time interactions, and behavioral observations. The system automatically drafts formal notification emails for your DCF Family Services Worker and GAL based on Vermont policy requirements.

4. Local & Offline Privacy Guarantee

All files and SQLite databases run 100% locally on your computer with zero external cloud transmission. In Sample Sandbox mode, private OneDrive files are completely shielded so personal information (SSNs, court orders) is never exposed.

5. System State, Architecture & Specifications

Authoritative documentation, database ERDs, API registries, and compliance checklists are maintained in the local repository:

  • Master System State: docs/SPROUT_SYSTEM_STATE.md (36 API routes, 12 views, 10 modals, 6 SQLite tables)
  • Documentation Catalog: docs/README.md (Full categorized index of all 42 specifications and guides)
  • Project Management Board: docs/PROJECT_MANAGEMENT_BOARD.md (Phases 1–12 sign-offs and roadmaps)
