### [00:00 - 01:30] PART 1: THE MISSION CONTROL COCKPIT
**SCREEN:** Mission Control (`mission_control`)  
**CLICK CUE:** None (start with hands free)  
**TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT:**  
"Hello, I’m Candy Chevalier. Welcome to Sprout, the Vermont Caregiver Operating System. 

Sprout was built around three guiding principles: to protect children, to reduce caregiver paperwork burden, and to ensure you are always organized and confident for court.

When you open Sprout, you land here at Mission Control. Look at the four quadrants: 
- In blue is **TRAIN**—our flight simulator for practice and training.
- In green is **CARE**—for your daily routines, medications, and school notes.
- In indigo is **DCF**—for court briefs and state reimbursement claims.
- In slate is **SYSTEM**—for offline settings and security.

No matter where you are in the system, clicking the Sprout leaf logo in the top corner will always bring you right back home."

**CLICK CUE:** Click Sprout logo to show safe return.

### [01:30 - 03:00] PART 2: PRIVACY SHIELD & PRACTICE SANDBOX
**SCREEN:** Top Header Bar  
**CLICK CUE:** Hover over `[Shield PII]`  
**TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT:**  
"Privacy comes first. Caregivers frequently share screens on Zoom or take screenshots. In the top right header, click **Shield PII**. Notice that all child dates, phone numbers, and notes blur instantly. 

Next, click the workspace toggle into the **Practice Sandbox**. Notice that our header turns navy blue with a yellow watermark line and a floating badge that says 'Practice Sandbox • Synthetic Data Only'. This is a flight simulator. You can test buttons, log sample notes, and explore without any risk of altering real child records."

**CLICK CUE:** Click `[Shield PII]`, then click `[Workspace Switch]`.

### [06:15 - 08:00] PART 5: TODAY'S PRIORITIES (MORNING COCKPIT)
**SCREEN:** Today Dashboard (`dashboard`)  
**CLICK CUE:** Click `Today` in main nav  
**TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT:**  
"Every morning, click **Today**. You see your morning command center: active children, medication doses due at 8:00 AM, today's 3:00 PM family visit, and the statutory health clock.

Under Vermont policy, every child placed in care must receive a comprehensive well-child medical exam within thirty days. Sprout's 30-Day Well-Child Health Exam clock counts down automatically so you never miss a state deadline. When you administer morning medication, check the box, and your digital log is complete."

**CLICK CUE:** Check off the 8:00 AM medication dose.

### [08:00 - 09:30] PART 6: 15-SECOND NOTE & $0.67/MILE MILEAGE
**SCREEN:** Log Note Modal  
**CLICK CUE:** Click `[+ Log Note]` (or press `Ctrl+K`)  
**TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT:**  
"Logging daily observations takes fifteen seconds. Click **Log Note**. Type what happened—or click **Voice Dictate** to speak directly into your microphone. 

If you drove the child to a doctor checkup, check Round-Trip Mileage. Click the 18-mile preset. Notice that Sprout automatically calculates your reimbursement at Vermont's official rate of sixty-seven cents per mile. Click Save, and your note is permanently archived."

**CLICK CUE:** Enter note, check mileage, click `18.0 mi` preset, highlight `($0.67/mi State Rate)`, click Save.

### [09:30 - 11:00] PART 7: CIRCLES OF CARE & LIFE JOURNEY
**SCREEN:** Circles of Care (`relationships`) ➔ Life Journey (`life_journey`)  
**CLICK CUE:** Click `Circles of Care` in nav  
**TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT:**  
"Caregiving is built on relationships. In **Circles of Care**, Sprout visualizes the child's support network into four concentric tiers: DCF workers, court advocates like the Guardian ad Litem, school teachers, and mentors.

Now click **Life Journey**. Children are whole human beings, not case files. Here we celebrate when a child makes the Honor Roll, scores a soccer goal, or finishes a chapter book. We also preserve comfort objects—like a favorite stuffed animal or bedtime routine. When a child reunifies with family or transitions to adoption, you can export a complete Child Lifebook packet."

**CLICK CUE:** Show Circles of Care visualizer, then click `Life Journey` and scroll milestone feed.
