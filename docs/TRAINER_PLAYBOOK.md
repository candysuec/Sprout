- [ ] Connect computer to presentation display / Zoom screen share.
- [ ] Open Sprout in browser.
- [ ] Click **`[Shield PII]`** in the header (ensures all names, phones, and dates are blurred for presentation safety).
- [ ] Click the workspace switcher to ensure **Practice Sandbox** is active (Navy blue header with yellow watermark line).

---

## 2. Standard 10-Minute Cohort Demonstration

### Minutes 0:00 – 2:00: The Safe Home & Mission Control
- Start at **Mission Control**. Point out the 3 core tenets: *Protect Children*, *Reduce Burden*, *Stay Prepared*.
- Explain the 4 quadrants: **TRAIN**, **CARE**, **DCF**, and **SYSTEM**.
- Demonstrate that clicking the Sprout leaf logo is the universal "safe home" action.

### Minutes 2:00 – 4:00: Persona Switching (15 Model Homes)
- Open **Training Hub** ➔ show the **15 Model Households**.
- Switch from Single Foster (`HH-101`) to Grandparents Kinship (`HH-113`).
- Show trainees how Sprout changes its language and rules dynamically to support kinship guardianship.

### Minutes 4:00 – 6:00: Crisis Simulation (11:00 PM Emergency Intake)
- Launch Scenario 1: *"11:00 PM Emergency Placement Intake"*.
- Walk through entering medication times and allergies in under 90 seconds.
- Click **`[Emergency Mode]`** in the header to show the 1-tap emergency hospital handoff card.

### Minutes 6:00 – 8:00: Automated Mileage Claims (Form FS-334B)
- Show how entering an 18-mile doctor trip automatically calculates reimbursement at \$0.67/mile.
- Click **`[Reports]`** ➔ demonstrate instant export of State Form `FS-334B` (.docx) with computer metadata pre-scrubbed.

### Minutes 8:00 – 10:00: Zero-Risk Guarantee & Trainee Hands-On
- Demonstrate the 1-click **Reset Sandbox to Factory Defaults** button.
- Reassure trainees: *"You cannot break this system. The sandbox is your flight simulator."*
- Invite trainees to open their laptops and follow the 5-minute self-guided orientation.
