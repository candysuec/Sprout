# CAREGIVER SCENARIO LIBRARY
## 9 Comprehensive Real-World Training Scenarios
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Date:** October 13, 2026

---

### Scenario A: New Foster Parent (Licensed Yesterday)
- **Situation:** Foster parent receives first placement call. Highly anxious about paperwork.
- **Goal:** Orient to Sprout in under 5 minutes without feeling overwhelmed.
- **Workflow:** Mission Control ➔ 5-Minute Tour ➔ Today Dashboard ➔ Note logging.
- **Expected Outcome:** Caregiver realizes Sprout automates deadlines; stress is significantly reduced.

---

### Scenario B: Kinship Grandparent (Sudden Placement)
- **Situation:** 58-year-old grandmother takes custody of 6-year-old grandson. Limited tech fluency.
- **Goal:** Track family time visits and understand DCF court steps without legal jargon.
- **Workflow:** Circles of Care ➔ Kinship Support Tab ➔ Juvenile Court Timeline Stepper.
- **Expected Outcome:** Clear plain-English understanding of guardianship steps.

---

### Scenario C: Respite Provider (72-Hour Weekend Stay)
- **Situation:** Provider receives child for Friday to Sunday care.
- **Goal:** Review allergies, bedtime comfort routines, and active meds without seeing biological court records.
- **Workflow:** Today Dashboard ➔ Life Journey Keepsakes ➔ Transport Signature Pad.
- **Expected Outcome:** Essential care is delivered smoothly; family confidentiality remains strictly protected.

---

### Scenario D: Emergency Placement (11:00 PM Intake)
- **Situation:** Emergency placement arrives late at night. Child is exhausted and tearful.
- **Goal:** Establish day 1 safety protocols in under 2 minutes.
- **Workflow:** Top Header ➔ `[Emergency Mode]` ➔ Review Allergies & Bedtime Meds ➔ Note comfort items.
- **Expected Outcome:** Caregiver has immediate hospital/police dossier ready; child is settled to sleep quickly.

---

### Scenario E: Court Review Next Week
- **Situation:** Family court permanency review hearing scheduled in 5 days.
- **Goal:** Provide comprehensive, objective care summary for Judge and Guardian ad Litem (GAL).
- **Workflow:** Reports ➔ Click `Court Review / GAL Stakeholder Brief` ➔ Download Word document (.docx).
- **Expected Outcome:** Pre-scrubbed judicial report compiled in seconds, saving 4+ hours of manual writing.

---

### Scenario F: Medication Refill Due
- **Situation:** Prescription bottle has only 3 days of doses remaining.
- **Goal:** Locate prescribing pediatrician and pharmacy details immediately.
- **Workflow:** Today Dashboard ➔ Health Domain ➔ Medication Administration Ledger.
- **Expected Outcome:** Prescribing physician phone number accessed in 2 clicks; refill requested on time.

---

### Scenario G: DCF Documentation Request
- **Situation:** DCF Family Services worker emails asking for last month's pediatric visits and school notes.
- **Goal:** Provide complete chronological records without sending confidential personal notes.
- **Workflow:** Reports ➔ Monthly Caregiver Summary ➔ Export.
- **Expected Outcome:** Professional, court-ready report sent to caseworker with verified dates.

---

### Scenario H: School Suspension / IEP Event
- **Situation:** School calls reporting child was sent to principal's office and suspended for 1 day.
- **Goal:** Document the school incident factually, avoid emotional blaming, and notify DCF team.
- **Workflow:** Fast Action Note (`Education` category) ➔ Type factual observation ➔ Flag follow-up needed.
- **Expected Outcome:** Objective record created; prompt notification sent to caseworker.

---

### Scenario I: Positive Milestone Celebration
- **Situation:** Child achieves 5th Grade Honor Roll and scores first goal in community soccer league.
- **Goal:** Celebrate and preserve positive growth in child's permanent Lifebook.
- **Workflow:** Life Journey ➔ `Celebrate Milestone` ➔ Tag category `Sports` / `Education` ➔ Save.
- **Expected Outcome:** Milestone permanently preserved in Child Lifebook for reunification or adoption handoff.
