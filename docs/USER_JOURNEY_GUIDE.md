# USER JOURNEY GUIDE
## Guided Pathways Across 7 Caregiver Roles
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Date:** October 13, 2026

---

### Pathway 1: New Foster Parent
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ 5-Minute Orientation ➔ Today's Priorities.
- **Learning Path:** Master the 4 Cognitive Domains (Health, Visits, Learning, Records).
- **Primary Workflows:** EPSDT 30-day health clock monitoring, school IEP notes, mileage logging.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Logging a 15-second daily behavioral observation and evening dose check.

---

### Pathway 2: Kinship Caregiver (Grandparent / Relative)
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ Circles of Care.
- **Learning Path:** Understanding kinship legal distinctions vs formal state custody.
- **Primary Workflows:** Tracking family time visitation, relative search notes, and legal guardianship court orders.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Documenting phone calls with biological parents and recording family visits.

---

### Pathway 3: Respite Provider
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ My Children ➔ Health & Wellbeing.
- **Learning Path:** Respite confidentiality and rapid routine comprehension.
- **Primary Workflows:** Reading child comfort objects/routines, verifying active medications, capturing transport signatures.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Reviewing bedtime routines, administering prescribed meds, logging emergency contacts.

---

### Pathway 4: Adoptive Placement
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ Life Journey Storybook.
- **Learning Path:** Transitioning from foster care to finalized permanency.
- **Primary Workflows:** Child Lifebook compilation, developmental milestones, and subsidy tracking.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Preserving childhood achievements, sports photos, and report card milestones.

---

### Pathway 5: State Trainer / VCWTP Instructor
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ Training Hub ➔ Trainer Demonstration Launcher.
- **Learning Path:** Navigating 15 synthetic household models and crisis simulation scenarios.
- **Primary Workflows:** Demonstrating 11 PM emergency intake, mileage reimbursement, and 1-click sandbox reset.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Projecting Sprout with `[Shield PII]` enabled to teach cohorts of prospective caregivers.

---

### Pathway 6: DCF Caseworker / Supervisor
- **Starting Point:** Mission Control ➔ Reports & Claims Center.
- **Learning Path:** Auditing caregiver compliance records.
- **Primary Workflows:** Reviewing pre-formatted court summaries, checking EPSDT screening dates, validating Form FS-334B claims.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Receiving monthly caregiver summary packets formatted for judge and GAL review.

---

### Pathway 7: Emergency Placement Intake
- **Starting Point:** Top Header ➔ Emergency Mode.
- **Learning Path:** Rapid child profile creation under urgent time constraints.
- **Primary Workflows:** Flagging allergies, noting emergency contacts, printing 1-tap hospital handoff cards.
- **Typical Daily Action:** Establishing day 1 safety protocols within 120 seconds of child arrival.
