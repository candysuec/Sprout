## 4. Certification Review 3: Video Walkthrough Simulation (Minute-by-Minute)

Candy should follow this exact **14-minute walkthrough sequence**:

### [0:00 – 1:30] Introduction & Mission Control
- **Screen:** Mission Control.
- **Talking Point:** *"Sprout is the Vermont Caregiver Operating System. It protects children, reduces paperwork burden, and ensures you are always prepared for court."*
- **Camera Action:** Highlight the 4 quadrants (**TRAIN**, **CARE**, **DCF**, **SYSTEM**).

### [1:30 – 3:00] Privacy Shield & Practice Sandbox
- **Screen:** Header.
- **Action:** Click **`[Shield PII]`**. Show the instant blur effect.
- **Talking Point:** *"If you are taking screenshots or sharing your screen on Zoom, one tap protects your family's personal details."*
- **Action:** Click **`[Workspace Switch]`** into **Practice Sandbox** (blue header turns on with yellow watermark strip).

### [3:00 – 4:45] Persona Switching (15 Model Households)
- **Screen:** Training Hub ➔ 15 Caregiver Profiles.
- **Action:** Switch from Sarah Jenkins (`HH-101`) to Grandparents Eleanor & Arthur Vance (`HH-113`).
- **Talking Point:** *"Sprout adapts its language whether you are a single foster parent or a kinship grandmother caring for your grandchildren."*

### [4:45 – 6:15] Emergency Mode (11:00 PM Intake Simulation)
- **Screen:** Top Header ➔ Click **`[Emergency Mode]`**.
- **Talking Point:** *"Imagine taking placement of an emergency child at 11:00 PM. One tap gives you an instant hospital handoff card with allergies, medications, and the 24/7 Vermont DCF Emergency Line."*

### [6:15 – 7:45] Today Dashboard (The Morning Cockpit)
- **Screen:** Today (`dashboard`).
- **Talking Point:** *"This is your morning command center. It shows medication doses due at 8:00 AM, today's family visit, and the 30-day health clock countdown."*
- **Action:** Check off the morning dose checkbox.

### [7:45 – 9:00] 15-Second Daily Note & Voice Dictation
- **Screen:** Fast Action Bar ➔ Click **`[+ Log Note]`** (or press `Ctrl+K`).
- **Talking Point:** *"Documenting behavior takes 15 seconds. You can type or use voice dictation. Notice how selecting an 18-mile doctor trip automatically calculates reimbursement at the state's official $0.67/mile rate."*

### [9:00 – 10:30] Circles of Care (Relationship Center)
- **Screen:** Circles of Care (`relationships`).
- **Talking Point:** *"Caregiving is about relationships. Sprout maps the child's support network into 4 clear tiers: Caseworkers, Legal Advocates, School & Pediatricians, and Mentors."*

### [10:30 – 11:45] Life Journey & Keepsakes (Trauma-Informed Lifebook)
- **Screen:** Life Journey (`life_journey`).
- **Talking Point:** *"Children are whole people, not case files. Sprout preserves soccer victories, honor roll awards, and favorite bedtime routines so their childhood story stays with them."*

### [11:45 – 13:00] Vermont DCF Reports & Mileage Export
- **Screen:** Reports (`reports`).
- **Action:** Click **`Generate Mileage Claim FS-334B`**.
- **Talking Point:** *"At the end of the month, one click compiles your mileage claim and court summary into pre-scrubbed Word documents."*

### [13:00 – 14:00] Safe Return Home & Conclusion
- **Screen:** Top Header ➔ Click Sprout Logo.
- **Talking Point:** *"No matter where you are in the application, clicking the Sprout logo returns you home to Mission Control. Consequence-free, private, and offline."*
