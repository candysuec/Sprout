# FAQ & COMMON QUESTIONS GUIDE
## Predictable Questions & Verified Answers for Live Demonstrations
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Date:** October 13, 2026

---

### Q1: "Can I install Sprout on my smartphone or iPad?"
> **Answer:** Yes. Sprout is engineered as an offline-first **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. In Safari or Chrome on your phone, tap *'Share'* ➔ *'Add to Home Screen'*. Sprout installs as an app with its own icon, works offline, and lets you log observations and mileage right from your car.

### Q2: "Can DCF caseworkers or state officials see my private notes?"
> **Answer:** No. Sprout runs 100% locally on your computer. Your raw notes never transmit to an external cloud or state database. You control what is shared by generating official exported reports (like Form FS-334B or Court Briefs) and emailing them directly to your caseworker.

### Q3: "What happens if I make a mistake while practicing in the Sandbox?"
> **Answer:** Nothing bad can happen! The Practice Sandbox is a safe flight simulator pre-loaded with synthetic model families. You can delete items, change doses, or test notes freely. With one click on *'Reset Sandbox to Factory Defaults'*, the training data resets instantly.

### Q4: "How does the $0.67/mile mileage rate get updated if the state changes it?"
> **Answer:** The mileage calculation engine is built with a configurable rate schedule. When Vermont DCF or the federal GSA updates the statutory reimbursement rate, Sprout updates the rate schedule automatically.

### Q5: "What if I lose my laptop or spill coffee on my computer?"
> **Answer:** In Sprout's Settings, there is a 1-click **Download Disaster Recovery Backup** button. It packages your entire SQLite database and documents into a secure `.zip` file that you can save to a thumb drive or secure external backup.
