## SECTION 1: TRAINING OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Purpose
To provide an instructor-ready curriculum and verbatim transcript enabling Candy Chevalier, Vermont Child Welfare Training Partnership (VCWTP) instructors, and DCF supervisors to train prospective foster parents, kinship caregivers, respite providers, and state caseworkers on Sprout / KinCare OS.

### 1.2 Target Audiences
- **New Foster Parents:** Needing clear daily operational workflows and deadline tracking.
- **Kinship Caregivers (Grandparents & Relatives):** Needing simplified, jargon-free support.
- **Respite Providers:** Needing 72-hour routine and allergy data without court records.
- **VCWTP Instructors:** Demonstrating modern caregiving technology in classroom settings.
- **DCF Family Services Staff:** Reviewing documentation accuracy and claims compliance.

### 1.3 Expected Learning Outcomes
By the end of the session, participants will be able to:
1. Navigate Sprout via **Mission Control** and return safely home at any time using the leaf logo.
2. Distinguish between the **Practice Sandbox** (flight simulator) and **Real Family Workspace**.
3. Shield sensitive child identity details using **`[Shield PII]`** and Child Reference IDs.
4. Check off daily medications and monitor statutory health clocks on the **Today Dashboard**.
5. Record a 15-second daily observation and log mileage at Vermont's **\$0.67/mile** state rate.
6. Generate pre-scrubbed court summaries and State Claim Form **FS-334B**.
7. Preserve childhood milestones and routines in the **Life Journey Storybook**.

### 1.4 Training Module Formats
- **5-Minute Overview:** High-level orientation for state leadership, DCF executives, and conferences.
- **15-Minute Demonstration:** Official walkthrough recording and cohort intro.
- **30-Minute Caregiver Training:** Standard orientation for new foster and kinship placements.
- **60-Minute Instructor Session:** Deep-dive for VCWTP trainers on persona switching and crisis simulations.
- **90-Minute Comprehensive Workshop:** Hands-on training including emergency intakes and court preparation.

## SECTION 3: INSTRUCTOR TALKING POINTS

### Mandatory "DO SAY" Phrases:
- *"Sprout is your private caregiver command center."*
- *"Children are whole people, not administrative case files."*
- *"Sprout helps caregivers focus on loving children instead of fighting paperwork."*
- *"Clicking the Sprout logo is your universal safe home."*
- *"In the Practice Sandbox, you can explore freely without touching real data."*
- *"Surnames are shielded under the Child Reference ID standard."*

### Prohibited "DO NOT SAY" Phrases:
- ❌ *"Complex database architecture"* ➔ Say: *"Organized digital care binder."*
- ❌ *"Backend SQLite database"* ➔ Say: *"Your private offline computer storage."*
- ❌ *"Data schemas and controllers"* ➔ Say: *"Daily care tools."*
- ❌ *"You have to remember your EPSDT clock"* ➔ Say: *"Sprout watches your 30-day health deadline for you."*
- ❌ *"You need to install Python servers"* ➔ Say: *"Sprout runs privately and securely right on your machine."*
