# NEW USER QUICK-START GUIDE
## Self-Teaching Pathways: 5, 15, 30 & 60 Minutes
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Date:** October 13, 2026

---

## Level 1: 5-Minute Orientation (Just the Essentials)
1. **Understand Where You Are:** Look at the top bar. Green = Real Home, Blue = Practice Sandbox.
2. **Click Today:** See your morning briefing, today's medication times, and upcoming appointments.
3. **Log a Note:** Click `[+ Log Note]` (or press `Ctrl+K`), type a short observation, and save.
4. **Return Home:** Click the Sprout logo at any time to return to Mission Control.

---

## Level 2: 15-Minute Guided Tour (The 4 Cognitive Domains)
1. Complete Level 1.
2. Go to **My Children** (`children`):
   - **Domain 1 (Health):** Check immunizations and the EPSDT 30-day health clock countdown.
   - **Domain 2 (Visits):** View the family time schedule and transport mileage presets.
   - **Domain 3 (Learning):** View school contacts and IEP accommodations.
   - **Domain 4 (Records):** Explore the 20-tab Vermont standard care binder.

---

## Level 3: 30-Minute Learning Path (State Compliance & Claims)
1. Complete Levels 1 and 2.
2. Log an 18-mile medical transport trip.
3. Navigate to **Reports** (`reports`) and generate a pre-filled Vermont Form **FS-334B** Mileage Claim (\$0.67/mi).
4. Review the **CHINS Juvenile Court Stepper** to understand Vermont statutory milestones.
5. Create a 1-tap **Emergency Hospital Handoff Card**.

---

## Level 4: 60-Minute Full Exploration (Simulator Mastery)
1. Complete Levels 1, 2, and 3.
2. Navigate to **Training Hub** (`training`).
3. Switch between 3 different household personas (Grandparents `HH-113`, Single Foster `HH-101`, Sibling Home `HH-104`).
4. Run through Scenario 1 (*11 PM Emergency Intake*) and Scenario 4 (*Tomorrow's Court Review*).
5. Open the **Audit & Settings** page to inspect the SHA-256 blockchain verification and download a 1-click disaster recovery backup snapshot.
