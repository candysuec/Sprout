## 1. THE FOUNDER QUESTION: WHY ARE WE HAVING THE SAME CONVERSATION REPEATEDLY?

We conducted an **Orientation Review**, then a **Journey Map**, then a **Mental Model Decision**, then a **Mental Model Enforcement Sprint**, and then a **Concept Governance Register**.

Yet after all five efforts, the Founder still had to report:
> *"The visual hierarchy is still fighting the mental model. The child is not yet the undeniable center of gravity. Review Console still competes with operations. Gateway language still mixes training and evaluation concepts."*

### Why does this keep happening?
Because **we have been suffering from Taxonomy Inflation, Documentation Inflation, and Governance Inflation**.

Every time a semantic ambiguity was discovered, the AI did not prune complexity—**it invented another classification layer**.
- When an evaluator needed to see the software, we invented an "Evaluator Role."
- When a legislator needed an ROI brief, we invented a "Legislator Journey."
- When we realized external people don't log in, we invented an "Audience" category.
- When we realized people have different intents, we invented a "Purpose" category.

**The fatal mistake:** We kept confusing **"Who cares about Sprout in the real world"** with **"What the software actually does on screen."**

We stopped asking: **"What software decision depends on this?"**  
Instead, we asked: **"What name can we give this abstraction?"**

This report is the end of that cycle.

---

## 2. WHAT DRIFT KEEPS RECURRING & WHY

### Recurring Drift Pattern 1: Audience Inflation (External Spectators Becoming In-App Roles)
* **What keeps happening:** People who read policy briefs, fund grants, or teach classes (Legislators, Foundations, State IT, DCF Supervisors, Trainers) keep creeping into the software as "User Types", "Personas", "Journeys", and "Doorways".
* **Why it happens:** The AI assumes that because a person is mentioned in a requirement document, they must have a screen, a button, or a profile in the code.
* **The Reality:** In the real world, a legislator never logs in. A foundation director never logs in. A DCF supervisor never logs in. **They receive paper or Word (.docx) outputs generated by the caregiver.** Building software for spectators usurps the caregiver's screen.

### Recurring Drift Pattern 2: Tool Inflation (Meta-Utilities Competing with Daily Care)
* **What keeps happening:** Tools built for Candy or evaluators (Review Console, Persona Launcher, Feedback Modal, Reset Sandbox) keep getting styled with loud glowing gradients, primary buttons, and top-tier placement, competing with feeding an infant or administering an asthma inhaler.
* **Why it happens:** In development, the testing tool is what the developer clicks 50 times a day. The AI subconsciously elevates developer/reviewer tools to the visual center, pushing the child to the periphery.
* **The Reality:** A foster parent opening Sprout at 11:00 PM does not care about SHA-256 blockchains, 178 test suites, or resetting the database. They care about **Liam's fever** and **Liam's bedtime bottle**.

### Recurring Drift Pattern 3: The Child Becoming an Attribute Rather than the Center
* **What keeps happening:** The placed child gets rendered as a tiny dropdown select box inside a navigation bar, while system banners, mode toggles, and multi-door airlocks dominate the viewport.
* **Why it happens:** Programmers think of the child as a foreign key (`child_id = 201`) in an SQL query. 
* **The Reality:** Sprout exists solely because a child is in state custody and a family is caring for them. If the child is not the immediate, undeniable human center of the screen, Sprout has failed its fundamental purpose.

## 3. THE PRUNING KNIFE: VALIDATING CONCEPTS AGAINST ACTUAL DECISIONS

We will no longer ask: *"What category does this belong to?"*  
We will ask: **"What concrete software or operational decision depends on this? If none, cut it."**

| Category Examined | What decision depends on this? | What mistake does it prevent? | Operational Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ROLE** | **Security permissions, domain sovereignty, and UI rendering.** (e.g., Parent Participant cannot see court briefs; Respite cannot see case history; Foster parent sees daily MAR). | Prevents catastrophic legal breaches and child safety leaks. | **ESSENTIAL. KEEP.** |
| **STATE** | **Database routing and technical containment.** (e.g., `sample` routes to synthetic models; `local` routes to private SQLite; child `Active` vs `Former`). | Prevents live family data contamination and boundary leaks. | **ESSENTIAL. KEEP.** |
| **WORKSPACE** | **Navigation destination.** Where is the user working? (Daily Desktop vs Parent Portal vs Flight School). | Prevents mixing adult flight simulation with daily child operations. | **ESSENTIAL. KEEP.** |
| **TOOL** | **Dialog invocation.** An actionable modal that opens, completes an atomic task, and closes. | Prevents utility dialogs from ballooning into permanent screens. | **USEFUL. KEEP AS UTILITIES.** |
| **AUDIENCE** | **NONE.** The software does not change a single database record or render a single unique caregiver field based on whether a legislator or a donor is looking. | None. In fact, it *caused* drift by creating fake software roles. | ❌ **RETIRE FROM SOFTWARE.** |
| **WORKFLOW** | **NONE AS A TAXONOMY.** A workflow is just a multi-step task (e.g., intake wizard). It does not need a governance layer. | None. Treating workflows as taxonomy caused "Emergency Placement" to be labeled a Role. | ❌ **RETIRE FROM TAXONOMY.** |
| **PURPOSE** | **NONE.** The software does not care if Candy is clicking a button to "Demonstrate", "Evaluate", or "Validate". The software only checks: Is `sproutMode === 'sample'`? | None. Purpose lives in the human's mind, not in the code. | ❌ **RETIRE COMPLETELY.** |

### The Pruning Outcome:
We reduce 7 categories to **3 Operating Truths**:
1. **WHO ARE YOU?** &rarr; **ROLE** (Foster Caregiver, Kinship Caregiver, Parent Participant, Respite Provider).
2. **WHERE ARE YOU WORKING?** &rarr; **WORKSPACE** (Mission Control, Parent Workspace, Training Hub).
3. **WHAT ENVIRONMENT IS THIS?** &rarr; **STATE** (`sample` Sandbox vs `local` Real Family Data).

Everything else is either a **Tool** (a button/modal you click) or **Real-World Reality Outside Sprout** (audiences, purposes, politics).

## 5. WHAT CONCEPTS MUST BE RETIRED IMMEDIATELY

1. ❌ **RETIRE "Legislator View" / "Legislator Role" / "Legislator Doorway":**  
   Sprout does not build features for politicians. Sprout builds tools for caregivers. If a legislator wants to see Sprout, the presenter opens the 5-minute tour.
2. ❌ **RETIRE "DCF Supervisor Role" / "DCF Worker Role":**  
   State caseworkers and supervisors do not have logins in Sprout. Sprout is caregiver-held. They are recipients of documents exported by the caregiver.
3. ❌ **RETIRE "Emergency Placement" as a Persona:**  
   Emergency Placement is an urgent 11:00 PM intake workflow. It is not a person. Sarah Jenkins or Eleanor Vance executes it.
4. ❌ **RETIRE "New User" and "Demo User" as Personas:**  
   Familiarity is not an identity. A new foster parent is still a foster parent.
5. ❌ **RETIRE "Audience" and "Purpose" from Software Governance:**  
   These belong in marketing slide decks, not in the application architecture.

## 6. THE 10 THINGS EVERY FUTURE AI MUST NEVER FORGET

> [!CRITICAL]
> **READ THIS BEFORE GENERATING CODE, UI, OR REPORTS.**  
> These are not categories. These are immutable operational truths.

### 1. THE CHILD IS THE UNDENIABLE CENTER OF GRAVITY.
Everything in Sprout exists for the child. When a caregiver looks at the screen, they must see **Liam** or **Emma** first—their face, their age, their safety alerts, their comforting needs. The software chrome, technical toggles, and metadata must recede into the background.

### 2. PARENT PARTICIPANT IS AN OPERATING USER.
Biological parents are real, active users of Sprout working toward reunification. They have their own dedicated, dignity-first workspace (`Parent Workspace`). They are never spectators, and their data is fail-closed and strictly isolated from case notes.

### 3. SPROUT IS CAREGIVER-HELD, NOT AN ENTERPRISE STATE SYSTEM.
Sprout belongs to the foster mother, kinship grandmother, and respite provider. DCF caseworkers and supervisors **do not log in**. They receive verified, professional Word documents and mileage claims exported by the caregiver.

### 4. SPECTATORS (LEGISLATORS, DONORS, AUDITORS) DO NOT GET IN-APP PERSONAS.
Never invent a "Legislator Dashboard", a "Donor View", or an "Auditor Role". When external spectators evaluate Sprout, they watch a demonstration or review printed outputs. They never touch daily operational controls.

### 5. REVIEW MODE IS A QUIET DIRECTOR UTILITY, NOT A WORKSPACE.
Review Mode is Candy’s evaluation console. It contains test monitors, SHA-256 ledgers, and sandbox resets. It must **never** shout with loud glowing gradients, sit in the middle of daily navigation, or compete with everyday caregiver logging.

### 6. MISSION CONTROL IS THE CAREGIVER'S DAILY DESKTOP, NOT AN AIRLOCK.
Mission Control is where a caregiver lives. It is not an orientation billboard, not a marketing flyer, and not a curriculum directory. It displays Liam's day, today's medication schedule, and 6 tactile 1-tap care buttons.

### 7. TRAINING HUB IS FLIGHT SCHOOL, NOT A ROLE SWITCHER.
The Training Hub is where formal VCWTP adult education lives (curriculum modules, scenario simulations, 15 model homes). It is not where you go to pick a persona. Persona switching lives in the Persona Launcher tool.

### 8. SANDBOX SAFETY MUST BE COMMUNICATED IN ONE QUIET SENTENCE, NOT A BLOCKING WALL.
The Sandbox Gateway exists only to say: *"This is 100% synthetic and consequence-free."* It must never be a massive 35-button wall that pushes the child off the screen.

### 9. PERSONAS ARE ATOMIC HAT-SWITCHES.
When a user switches practice roles in the sandbox, Sprout atomically sets the caregiver and canonical child together:
- **Sarah Jenkins &rarr; Liam Brooks** (Infant, Foster Care)
- **Eleanor Vance &rarr; Emma Vance** (Toddler, Kinship Care)
- **Samantha Price &rarr; Liam Brooks** (Infant, Weekend Respite)
- **Angela Davis &rarr; Emma Vance** (Reunification Roadmap)  
Zero detective work. Zero manual child hunting.

### 10. NEVER INVENT A NEW CONCEPT WHEN YOU CAN PRUNE AN OLD ONE.
Whenever friction arises in navigation or mental models, the solution is **never** to create a new category, a new document, or a new layer. The solution is always to ask: *"What wrong idea made us build this? Cut it."*
