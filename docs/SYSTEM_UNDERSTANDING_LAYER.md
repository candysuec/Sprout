## 1. The Core Problem & North Star

Traditional software engineering focuses on feature accumulation. In high-stress caregiving environments (such as taking emergency custody of a distressed child at 11:00 PM), feature density without cognitive clarity causes user paralysis.

### The North Star
> **"A user should never have to ask: 'Where do I click?', 'Why is this here?', 'How does this work?', or 'What do I do next?' Sprout should teach itself."**

---

## 2. The 6 Foundational Questions Answered

### Question 1: Where am I?
- Look at the top navigation header:
  - **Deep Emerald Green Header:** You are in the **Real Family Workspace** (Local Live Data). Any notes, medication records, or appointments you log are encrypted and stored in your private local database.
  - **Navy Blue Header with Amber Line:** You are in the **Practice Sandbox**. You are working with synthetic training data (Sarah Jenkins `HH-101`, Marcus Vance `HH-102`, etc.). You can experiment, make mistakes, and practice freely without affecting real children.

### Question 2: What is Sprout?
- Sprout is a private, offline-first operating system designed specifically for Vermont foster, kinship, respite, and adoptive families operating within the DCF Family Services ecosystem.
- It automates state documentation (FS-334B mileage claims, EPSDT 30-day health screenings, court reports, and medication logs) so caregivers can focus on caring for children rather than fighting paperwork.

### Question 3: What should I do next?
- If you have an active placement today: Click **`[Today]`** to see your morning briefing, today's medication times, and upcoming appointments.
- If you just took an emergency placement: Click **`[Emergency Mode]`** or **`[+ New Child]`** to record immediate allergies, doctor contacts, and create a 1-tap hospital handoff card.
- If you are learning the system: Click **`[Mission Control]`** ➔ **`[5-Minute New User Orientation]`**.

### Question 4: Which experience applies to me?
- Review the **7 Role-Based Guided Pathways** in Mission Control:
  - **New Foster Parent:** Focus on 30-day health clock compliance and school IEP communication.
  - **Kinship Caregiver:** Focus on relative search documentation and legal guardianship petitions.
  - **Respite Provider:** Focus on weekend medication schedules and allergy/routine alerts.
  - **Adoptive Parent:** Focus on Child Lifebook preservation and permanency court records.
  - **Trainer / Supervisor:** Focus on demonstrating the 15 model homes and crisis simulations.

### Question 5: How do I learn the system?
- Use the **Training Hub (`training`)**. Run through the 6 real-world simulation scenarios (11 PM Emergency Intake, Missed Parent Visit, Med Refill Crisis, Tomorrow's Court Review).
- Practice logging mileage trips to see how Sprout automatically calculates the \$0.67/mile state rate.

### Question 6: How do I start working?
- Click the workspace switcher in the header to ensure **Real Family Workspace** is selected.
- Click **`[+ Log Note]`** or press `Ctrl+K` to record an observation, doctor appointment, or school event.
