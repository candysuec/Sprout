- [ ] **Display Setup:** Connect laptop to presentation display or Zoom share.

- [ ] **Activate Sandbox:** Switch workspace to **Practice Sandbox** (verify navy blue header and yellow watermark line).
- [ ] **Load Initial Persona:** Set caregiver to **Sarah Jenkins (`HH-101`)**.

## 2. Live Demo Script Pause Points & Audience Prompts

### Pause Point 1 (Minute 1:30 - Mission Control)
- **Trainer Prompt:** *"Before I click anything, look at these four quadrants. Is it obvious to everyone where you would start if you were a new foster parent? In blue is practice, green is daily care."*
- **Listen For:** Heads nodding; audience recognizing clear color categorization.

### Pause Point 2 (Minute 5:00 - Emergency Mode)
- **Trainer Prompt:** *"Has anyone here ever taken an emergency intake late at night? How long did it take you to find the medication schedule or the DCF after-hours line?"*
- **Contrast:** *"In Sprout, that entire packet is ready in two seconds."*

### Pause Point 3 (Minute 8:45 - Mileage Calculation)
- **Trainer Prompt:** *"Notice the mileage calculation. At $0.67 per mile, an eighteen-mile round trip is twelve dollars and six cents. How many of you still use paper mileage slips or forget to submit claims at the end of the month?"*
- **Value Point:** Sprout puts money directly back into caregiver households.

### Pause Point 4 (Minute 10:45 - Life Journey & Milestones)
- **Trainer Prompt:** *"When children leave your home—whether returning to parents or moving to an adoptive family—what happens to their artwork, sports certificates, and bedtime traditions?"*
- **Value Point:** Sprout exports a physical or digital Lifebook so no child loses their personal history.
