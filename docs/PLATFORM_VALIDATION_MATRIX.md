# PLATFORM VALIDATION MATRIX
## Systematic Evaluation of Every Sprout Hub & Cognitive Domain
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Reference  
**Date:** October 13, 2026

---

| Hub / Domain | Why Does it Exist? | Who Benefits? | When is it Used? | How Often? | What Problem Does it Solve? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mission Control** | Universal safe home & system orientation | All users | Upon initial launch & whenever lost | Multiple times daily | Eliminates navigation confusion & cognitive paralysis. |
| **Today Dashboard** | Morning command center & daily briefing | Primary Caregiver | Morning, afternoon, and bedtime | 3-5 times daily | Surfaces medications due, today's visits, and health clocks. |
| **My Children** | Comprehensive child dossier & 4 domains | Caregivers & caseworkers | Reviewing medical, education, or visit history | Daily / Weekly | Consolidates scattered files into structured profiles. |
| **Health Domain** | Prescriptions, allergies, well-child clock | Caregiver, Doctor | Administering meds & tracking checkups | Daily | Prevents missed doses and enforces EPSDT 30-day compliance. |
| **Visits Domain** | Supervised family time & transport | Caregiver, Bio Parents | Scheduling and documenting family visits | 1-3 times weekly | Tracks visit attendance, transport mileage, and reactions. |
| **Learning Domain**| School contacts, IEP/504 goals, grades | Caregiver, Teacher | Documenting school meetings & homework | 2-4 times weekly | Unifies special ed accommodations and teacher communications. |
| **Care Binder** | 20-tab Vermont standard digital binder | Caregiver, DCF Auditor | Storing vital records, court orders, agreements | As documents arrive | Eliminates messy paper binders and lost state documents. |
| **Circles of Care**| 4-tier stakeholder relationship map | Caregiver, GAL, Worker | Tracking stakeholder calls and follow-ups | 2-3 times weekly | Prevents lost emails and documents every caseworker contact. |
| **Life Journey** | Trauma-informed Lifebook & celebrations | Child, Caregiver, Family | Celebrating honors, sports wins, routines | Weekly | Preserves childhood identity and prevents lost history. |
| **Training Hub** | Consequence-free caregiver flight simulator| Prospective parents | Self-study, classroom instruction | During training | Allows learning without fear of breaking real children's data. |
| **Reports** | Automated court briefs & state claims | Caregiver, Court, DCF | End of month or prior to hearings | Monthly | Eliminates hours of manual writing; formats Form FS-334B. |
| **Settings** | SHA-256 blockchain ledger & backups | Technical audit, Family | System checks and disaster recovery | Monthly | Provides tamper-proof audit defense and offline data security. |
