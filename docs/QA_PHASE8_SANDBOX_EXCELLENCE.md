## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

In Phase 8, the Sprout Practice Sandbox was elevated from a static sample-dataset viewer into a **state-of-the-art Caregiver Flight Simulator and Training Hub**.

The system enables prospective foster parents, kinship caregivers, respite providers, adoptive placements, and emergency homes to make mistakes, practice high-stress intakes, rehearse court preparation, and learn objective, non-judgmental documentation—**without any risk of touching or compromising real family data**.
